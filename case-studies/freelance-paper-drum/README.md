# Paper Drum Manufacturer — Competitive Intelligence & Pricing Strategy

## Freelance Project Demo

**Market Research · Competitive Intelligence · Pricing Strategy**

> **Important:** This is a demonstration case study created as part of my freelance project work. It is **not a direct client deliverable**. Client identity and underlying financial information are withheld for confidentiality.

### Project Overview

A demonstration of an end-to-end competitive intelligence and market research engagement for a mid-sized fibre-drum manufacturer. The project examined the competitive landscape, product differentiation, customer purchase criteria and pricing position to identify opportunities for profitable growth.

### Work Demonstrated

- Competitor research and benchmarking across product specifications and indicative pricing
- Market and industry research across fibre drums and paper packaging
- Customer/buyer purchase-criteria analysis
- Porter’s Five Forces and SWOT analysis
- Product differentiation and value-proposition development
- Tiered, benchmark-anchored pricing strategy
- Export-packaging opportunity assessment
- Strategic recommendations and phased implementation roadmap

### Outcome Framework

The case study demonstrates how research findings were translated into a differentiated positioning and pricing strategy, with outcomes represented as indexed metrics to preserve confidentiality.
