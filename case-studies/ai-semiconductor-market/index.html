02 · Forecast interpretation

Growth remains positive, while annual growth decelerates

The baseline adds 2,217 units per year, so the model is built around a linear increase in absolute value. Because the base grows each year, the implied year-on-year growth rate declines from approximately 50.0% in 2021 to 8.3% in 2031. This is a property of the workbook's construction, not independent proof of the real market's growth path.

Linear absolute growth

Annual increments remain constant in the top-line series. This is easy to audit but may not reflect market cycles, supply constraints or changing demand drivers.

Decelerating YoY rate

Percentage growth falls mechanically as the same absolute increment is applied to a larger base. A compound-growth scenario should be tested alongside this baseline.

Forecast horizon

Use 2020–2023 as historical only if validated against cited sources. Label later years as estimates or forecasts and record the forecast cut-off date.

04 · Business implications

What a decision-maker should take away

Use the workbook as a scenario-planning scaffold, not yet as an externally validated market forecast. The most useful next step is to validate the total market baseline and each segment-share curve against dated primary or reputable secondary sources, then compare linear, compound-growth and downside cases.
  • Investment screening: Compare segment growth and market size only after confirming definitions, units and data provenance.
  • Go-to-market planning: Treat technology and deployment shares as hypotheses to test, not facts to repeat in an executive presentation.
  • Forecast governance: Keep source, date, unit, formula and confidence level alongside each input.
05 · Model quality & next steps

Make the model decision-ready

Document

Record the definition of “AI semiconductor market,” unit, geographic scope, inclusion/exclusion rules, source URLs and actual-vs-forecast split.

Stress-test

Add conservative/base/upside cases, variable annual growth, and segment-share checks that must sum to 100% each year.

Communicate

Add a trend chart, a segment-mix chart, a concise assumptions table and a visible note distinguishing sourced facts from model assumptions.
