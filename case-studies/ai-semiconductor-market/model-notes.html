AI Semiconductor Market — Model Notes

Workbook: AI Semiconductor Market.xlsx · United States · Annual model 2020–2031

Purpose and workbook structure

The single Sizing sheet contains a top-line annual series, year-on-year growth calculations, segment-value formulas and year-specific segment-share assumptions.

  • Top line: row 7; starts at 4,435 in 2020 and increases by 2,217 workbook units per year to 28,822 in 2031.
  • YoY growth: row 8, current-year value divided by prior-year value minus one.
  • Segment values: segment share multiplied by the top-line value for that year.
  • Share assumptions: columns S:AD; residual categories are calculated as 100% minus the other listed shares.
  • CAGR: column P uses Excel RATE over the selected 2027–2031 interval.

Observed model behaviour

  • Absolute annual growth is constant at 2,217 units, making the top-line construction linear.
  • Implied YoY growth declines from approximately 50.0% in 2021 to 8.3% in 2031.
  • Calculated 2027–2031 CAGR is approximately 9.63%.
  • From 2020 to 2031, the assumed ML share rises 25.0% → 26.2%; Deep Learning 16.0% → 17.3%; NLP falls 31.0% → 28.6%.
  • Hybrid remains the largest deployment share at about 53.9% in 2031; Services remain the largest product category at about 56.3%; Integrated Device Manufacturers remain the largest end-user category at about 61.4%.

Data limitations — must resolve before external use

The workbook does not specify the unit/currency for its top-line values, source URLs, publication dates or actual-versus-forecast cut-off. Do not present the numbers as USD or verified market revenue until those details are confirmed. Segment shares should be sourced or explicitly labelled as scenarios.

Validation checklist

  • Define market boundary, value chain, geography and inclusion/exclusion rules.
  • Add source, source date, unit and rationale for every key input.
  • Separate observed historical values from forecast years.
  • Add conservative, base and upside scenarios, including a compound-growth alternative to the linear baseline.
  • Check segment shares total 100% each year and segment values reconcile to the total.
  • Add a top-line trend chart, segment mix chart and model version/date.

Decision-use conclusion

This is a useful spreadsheet scaffold for sizing and segment allocation, but it is not yet suitable for a client-facing market-size claim until units, data provenance and forecast assumptions are validated.
