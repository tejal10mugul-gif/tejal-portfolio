# Four-Part Market Intelligence Analysis

**Independent Demonstration Project · Not a Client Deliverable**

A four-part market intelligence project analysing labour-market conditions, global workforce pools, AI-skill intensity and India's software-developer supply. The work follows a structured **Research → Validate → Analyse → Interpret** approach and translates data into practical market and sourcing implications.

### Four analytical areas

- **European Labour Markets:** unemployment and employment momentum across the EU, Poland, Czechia and Germany, highlighting differences in labour-market tightness.
- **Global Workforce Pools:** workforce scale, competitiveness and compensation comparisons across major markets, with India emerging as the strongest overall balance in the supplied dataset.
- **AI Skills & Workforce:** country-level AI-skill penetration and global programming-language signals, including implications for AI/data talent sourcing.
- **India Developer Supply:** a bottom-up guesstimate of India's software-developer addressable pool, estimated at approximately 4–6 million with a central case of ~5.0 million.

Market Intelligence · Competitive Intelligence · Secondary Research · Data Interpretation · Workforce Analytics · Market Sizing & Guesstimation · Strategic Insight Development · Executive Communication
