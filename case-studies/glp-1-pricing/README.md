# GLP-1 & Weight-Loss Drug Pricing Intelligence Brief

## Self-Directed Market Research & Competitive Intelligence Project

**Pharma & Life Sciences Pricing Analysis · Market Intelligence · Competitive Intelligence**

A self-directed pricing intelligence brief built in the style of an executive client deliverable. The project analyses branded GLP-1 pricing across cash-pay, insurance and global markets, with a focus on pricing architecture, access, policy and competitive dynamics.

### What the project covers

- US list/WAC price benchmarking across major branded GLP-1 products
- List price vs. self-pay vs. payer/copay price-cascade analysis
- Manufacturer direct-to-consumer pricing and savings-card dynamics
- Medicare, Medicaid and commercial payer access considerations
- IRA negotiation, MFN pricing and policy-driven price changes
- International price benchmarking across peer markets
- India semaglutide patent-expiry and generic price compression
- Competitive comparison of Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly pricing strategies
- Oral GLP-1 entrants and implications for category pricing
- Stakeholder-specific strategic pricing implications

### Research approach

The brief prioritises primary government, regulatory and company sources, including CMS, FDA, White House/HHS materials and company filings/press releases, supplemented by clearly identified research and wire sources where appropriate. The methodology and source trail are documented in the presentation.
