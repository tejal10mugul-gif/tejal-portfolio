# Oncology Sales Forecasting Model

**Excel Forecasting Model · Oncology · Commercial & Pharma Forecasting**

A structured forecasting model for a hypothetical first-line lung-cancer therapy, designed to estimate patient pool, treatment flow and drug sales through 2030. The workbook uses epidemiology and treatment assumptions to build a monthly forecasting model and translate patient dynamics into commercial sales estimates.

### What it demonstrates
- Target patient pool estimation and disease-progression logic
- Monthly epidemiology and treatment funnel modelling
- Population, diagnosis and treatment-rate assumptions
- NSCLC subtype and eligible-patient calculations
- Patient flow translated into treated-patient and sales forecasts
- Forecasting through 2030 with linked calculation sheets
- Structured Excel modelling for commercial/pharma forecasting

**Skills:** Commercial Forecasting · Oncology Market Research · Epidemiology Modelling · Patient Funnel Analysis · Excel Financial/Forecast Modelling

**Note:** This is a portfolio forecasting model based on a hypothetical drug scenario; it is not a client deliverable.
