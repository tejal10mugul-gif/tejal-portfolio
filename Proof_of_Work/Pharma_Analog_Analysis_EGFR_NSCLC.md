# Pharma Analog Analysis – EGFR-Mutated Metastatic NSCLC

## Proof of Work | Market Intelligence & Forecasting

### Objective

This case study demonstrates an **analog-based commercial forecasting approach** for a hypothetical drug entering a new first-line (1L) setting in the US market.

**Target drug:** Hypothetical Drug X  
**Disease:** EGFR-mutated metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC)  
**Biomarker:** EGFR exon 19 deletion / exon 21 L858R  
**New setting:** First-line (1L) metastatic NSCLC  
**Geography:** US  
**Illustrative launch year:** 2027

### Business questions

1. How quickly could the target drug be adopted after launch?
2. When could it reach peak uptake?
3. What could its market-share trajectory look like over the first five years?

---

## 1. Market Definition

The forecast market is defined using:

- Disease: metastatic NSCLC
- Biomarker: EGFR Ex19del / L858R
- Line of therapy: 1L
- Geography: US
- Treatment class: oral EGFR-targeted therapy

The market definition is critical because analogs should be selected against the **target drug's characteristics**, not simply against the broad disease.

**Disease + Biomarker + Line of Therapy + Geography + Treatment Class**

Additional qualitative factors to assess before finalizing the analog set:

- Efficacy
- Safety/tolerability
- Route and dosing convenience
- Treatment positioning
- Unmet medical need

## 4. Benchmark Calculation

The benchmark is based on the **median analog market share** for each post-launch year.

Example:

**Year 3**

Analog shares:

- Osimertinib = 19%
- Afatinib = 16%
- Gefitinib = 13%
- Erlotinib = 13%
- Dacomitinib = 11%

Median benchmark = **13%**

The target-drug forecast can then be adjusted based on the target product's expected differentiation.

### Simple formula

**Target Share = Median Analog Share + Target-specific Adjustment**

This adjustment should be supported by assumptions around:

- Clinical differentiation
- Safety
- Convenience
- Competitive intensity
- Physician adoption
- Access/reimbursement

## 6. Commercial Forecast Logic

The commercial bridge is:

**Addressable Patient/Market Pool**  
→ **Target Drug Adoption**  
→ **Market Share**  
→ **Revenue**

Simple revenue formula:

**Target Sales = Addressable Market × Target Market Share**

Example:

If the addressable market is **$2,000M** and target share is **15%**:

**Target Sales = $2,000M × 15% = $300M**

---

## 7. Key Learning

Analog analysis is a **benchmark-based forecasting methodology**, not an exact prediction.

The objective is to use comparable historical market behavior to develop a **structured and defensible assumption** for:

- Early uptake
- Growth trajectory
- Peak market share
- Time to peak
- Mature market position
- Potential sales

The strongest analog forecast is not necessarily the one with the most sophisticated model. It is the one with:

**Clear market definition + defensible analog selection + transparent assumptions + appropriate adjustments.**
