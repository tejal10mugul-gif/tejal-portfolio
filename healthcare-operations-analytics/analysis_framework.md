# Analysis Framework — Tejal Mugul

## 1. Demand
Track patient volume by month, age group and demographic segment.

## 2. Flow
Examine referral patterns, admissions and movement through the emergency-care journey.

## 3. Timeliness
Measure average wait time and the proportion of patients receiving timely care.

## 4. Experience
Review patient satisfaction alongside service-timeliness indicators.

## 5. Management implications
Translate patterns into questions such as:
- Are specific periods showing higher operational pressure?
- Where is timeliness weakest?
- Which segments require closer monitoring?
- Is lower satisfaction associated with service delays?
- Which departments/referral pathways appear to require process review?

## 6. Decision output
Produce a concise management view:
**What happened → Where → Why it may matter → What to investigate next**
