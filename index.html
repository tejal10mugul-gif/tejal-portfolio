About

I work at the intersection of research and strategy — translating complex market data into decision-ready intelligence that drives business action. My work spans competitive benchmarking, market sizing, and executive reporting, always anchored in the question: what does this mean for the business?

With coverage across ICT, Chemicals, Automotive, Industrial, and Consumer sectors, I bring a structured analytical lens to every engagement. Frameworks like SWOT, PESTLE, and Porter's Five Forces are tools for genuine strategic thinking — helping stakeholders understand competitive dynamics, regulatory environments, and the market forces that shape outcomes.

I bring cross-border research depth across North America, Europe, APAC, LATAM, and MEA — with a track record of translating regional market structures into unified, decision-ready intelligence for Fortune 500 clients. Combined with strong stakeholder collaboration skills, this enables me to bridge research depth with clear business narrative.

Market research gave me a strong analytical foundation, but I realized I enjoy using insights to solve business problems even more than generating them. That's what draws me to consulting. I enjoy breaking down complex business challenges, working across different industries, and developing evidence-based recommendations that help clients make better strategic decisions. The opportunity to continuously learn, collaborate with diverse stakeholders, and create tangible business impact is what makes consulting the career I want to build.

Market Forecasting

From market size to market forecast

A forecast is only useful if the decision-maker can see what it assumes. This is how I build one, and a small model you can change yourself.

1

Size the base year

Build the starting market bottom-up and top-down, then reconcile the two with TAM, SAM and SOM.

2

Name the growth drivers

Demand, pricing, regulation, technology and adoption. Each driver gets a stated assumption and a source.

3

Run three scenarios

Base, upside and downside cases, so the range of outcomes is visible instead of a single number.

4

Test it against reality

Compare the output with company filings, analogue markets and expert input before it reaches a deck.

Currently deepening demand modelling for pharma and cross-industry markets through Forecastify Academy (in progress).
